title: "Geodesic Motion on SO(3)"
---
The special orthogonal group denoted by SO(3) is the Lie group of rotation matrices in three-dimensional Euclidean space defined by 

$$
SO(3)= {R\in\mathbb{R}^{3\times3}\mid R^\top R=I_3,\ \det(R)=1}
$$

The free rigid body's motion can be viewed as geodesic motion on SO(3) equipped with a left-invariant Riemannian metric.
